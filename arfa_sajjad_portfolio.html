01 / About Me

Creative direction with a complete visual world.

I’m Arfa Sajjad. I’m a creative designer with a strong interest in fashion, visual identity and digital design.

My design approach is driven by elegance, detail and a clear visual identity. I’m especially drawn to refined luxury, dramatic silhouettes, rich textures, jewellery-inspired details and sophisticated colour palettes.
